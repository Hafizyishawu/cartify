Your assurance level is the strength of the identity claim recorded on work you register. Raising it changes what future records say. It cannot reach back and change one already in the log.

Prove a domain

Publish a TXT record on a domain you control. A buyer who recognises your-studio.com learns more from that than from an opaque account identifier, which is the whole reason this rung exists.

The check queries two independent DNS resolvers and requires both to return the same record. One resolver answering alone is what a poisoned cache looks like, and it is also what ordinary mid-propagation DNS looks like. Neither is proof.
